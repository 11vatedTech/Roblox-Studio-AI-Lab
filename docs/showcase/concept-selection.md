## Selected direction: Signal Garden

The strongest demonstration is a cooperative exploration puzzle where players gather signal fragments, route them through a spatial network, and wake distinct marsh habitats. Routing changes the traversable world and the mix of hazards, so replay decisions matter. A complete slice should show server-authoritative collection, a cooperative circuit objective, readable UI, distinct environmental landmarks, lighting and audio transitions, and a compact replay loop.

Signal Garden is now implemented as a source-controlled cooperative playable slice with native Terrain/Part scenery, shared collection and deposit, two route choices, a timed two-plate relay, environment response, HUD feedback, and a replay reset. The generated place opened in Studio and started in local play mode. The environment is still a stylized blockout. Full objective interaction, multiplayer, server-log review, MCP-based viewport evidence, and device profiling remain open validation gates; the concept scores remain design estimates rather than player validation.
