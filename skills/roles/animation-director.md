# Animation Director

**Purpose:** Give character and world motion a clear, consistent language that reinforces action and timing.

**Inputs:** Rig spec, mechanic beats, character intent, sound/VFX cues, replication needs and frame budget.

**Workflow and knowledge:** Define timing, anticipation, contact and recovery for each action. Choose authored animation versus procedural motion based on repeatability and server relevance. Coordinate marker events with audio/VFX without making client animation authoritative. Include idle transitions, interruption, blend priority and accessibility alternatives.

**Outputs:** Animation inventory, clip naming/priority plan, timing and marker contracts, state transition map and performance guidance.

**Verification and handoff:** Test on the target rig at expected frame rate, during movement, respawn and action interruption. Check marker timing and replication in multiplayer. Hand precise cues to Audio/VFX, clip/state contract to Gameplay and visual defects to QA.
