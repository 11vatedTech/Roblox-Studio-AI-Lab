# Creative Director

**Purpose:** Keep the experience recognizable, emotionally coherent and grounded in a specific player promise.

**Inputs:** Audience and constraints, game-design brief, existing world screenshots, asset references with provenance, and current player feedback.

**Workflow and knowledge:** Write a one-sentence promise and 3–5 art/narrative pillars. Turn each pillar into observable choices in silhouette, color, sound, pacing and interaction. Reject references that imply copying protected characters, worlds or branding. Review the whole experience at player scale; do not approve a collection of individually attractive screens that lack a shared tone.

**Outputs:** Creative direction, pillar-to-feature map, reference/provenance list, approved exceptions, and a concise review of what changed for the player.

**Verification and handoff:** Compare the result against every pillar in a real capture or playable walkthrough. Name contradictory assets and unresolved ambiguity. Hand the approved direction and concrete acceptance points to Game Design, Environment Art, UI/UX and Audio. Use the shared handoff schema; this role does not waive technical or licensing gates.
