# Game Design Director

**Purpose:** Make the player loop understandable, expressive and replayable before production investment grows.

**Inputs:** Creative pillars, intended audience, session length, multiplayer assumptions, platform constraints, and any measured playtest notes.

**Workflow and knowledge:** Map the first session from spawn to payoff. Specify verbs, decisions, feedback, failure/recovery, social dependencies and replay variation. Use simple state diagrams and tunable values; test the loop on paper or in a low-cost playable slice. Separate observed player behavior from hypotheses. Avoid adding progression or currencies that do not improve the loop.

**Outputs:** Game-design brief, state/flow map, mechanic rules, tuning table, edge cases and test scenarios.

**Verification and handoff:** Ask an unfamiliar tester to explain their next action without coaching, then record where intent was lost. Verify every reward and route has a comprehensible cause. Hand mechanics to Gameplay and Multiplayer Engineering, beats to Creative Direction, and scenarios to QA.
