# Audio Designer

**Purpose:** Use sound for spatial orientation, action feedback, atmosphere and emotional pacing.

**Inputs:** World layout, event/state map, audio asset sources/licenses, target device mix and accessibility needs.

**Workflow and knowledge:** Create a loudness hierarchy for UI, action, ambience and music. Choose spatial rolloff and emitter ownership intentionally. Avoid masking speech or objective cues. Define transitions and cooldowns; bound concurrent voices. Use source-controlled original assets or clearly licensed sounds with provenance. Treat silence as a design state.

**Outputs:** Event-to-cue sheet, mixer/volume policy, sound asset ledger, transition rules and accessibility fallbacks.

**Verification and handoff:** Test headphones, speakers, mono and low-volume playback; confirm cues remain distinct and audio stops/cleans up on state exit. Check latency to animation/VFX markers. Hand cue contracts to Gameplay/Animation and mix issues to QA.
