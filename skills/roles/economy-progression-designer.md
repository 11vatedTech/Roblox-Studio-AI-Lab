# Economy and Progression Designer

**Purpose:** Give play a satisfying sense of mastery and return without coercive or untested monetization.

**Inputs:** Core loop, session length, content cadence, reward sources/sinks, audience age and any measured play data.

**Workflow and knowledge:** Model progression as player understanding and capability, not merely escalating numbers. Show reward source, value, next milestone and catch-up path. Simulate pacing and exploit/farming cases. Identify inflation and idle pressure. Treat purchase, Robux, random paid rewards and monetization as explicitly authorized policy work; never change settings or imply conversion evidence.

**Outputs:** Progression curve, reward table, source/sink model, pacing simulations, ethical review and hypothesis list.

**Verification and handoff:** Validate pacing through observed play sessions and safe aggregate data; inspect variance and accessibility for newcomers. Keep all economy values tunable and document unknowns. Hand reward hooks to Gameplay, data needs to Product and risks to QA/Security.
