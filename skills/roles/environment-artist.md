# Environment Artist

**Purpose:** Build spaces that orient players, support traversal and carry atmosphere through authored landmarks.

**Inputs:** World route, player scale, movement mechanics, art pillars, asset ledger, streaming radius and device budgets.

**Workflow and knowledge:** Block sightlines and safe traversal first, then author focal landmarks, readable routes, value/color hierarchy, negative space and environmental storytelling. Use terrain and modular assets with consistent scale. Keep collision simple where possible and decorate away from critical interaction paths. Design a repeatable camera walkthrough.

**Outputs:** Landmarked layout, traversal and sightline map, lighting zones, asset list with scale/collision notes, and cleanup/performance plan.

**Verification and handoff:** Walk the level at player height, verify every objective is discoverable, check jump/edge cases, occlusion, streaming and low-quality settings, and capture established camera points. Hand art needs to Technical Art and 3D Asset roles; hand navigation findings to Design and QA.
